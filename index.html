HOW TO PLAY
⛏ GROTTO TRIALS — RULES & MECHANICS ⛏
🔥 FREE TRIAL MODE
1
You are shown an image of a HashCash miner NFT — identify it by choosing the correct name from 3 options (pillars A, B, C or keys 1/2/3).
2
You have 3 lives. A wrong answer or timeout costs one life. Lose all three and the Grotto claims you.
3
There are 10 rounds per game. Difficulty escalates: Easy → Medium → Hard → Extreme. The timer shrinks each tier.
4
Answer faster for time bonus points. Chain correct answers for a streak multiplier (up to ×5). Every 5th round is a double-points Speed Bonus Round.
5
Press H or the Hint button once per game to eliminate one wrong option at the cost of 30 points.
⚔ INFERNO TRIAL (PAID MODE)
1
Pay $1 worth of $HERESY (0.00085 HERESY on The Grotto L1) or $1 worth of $HCASH (18 HCASH on Avalanche) to enter.
2
Each $1 entry gives you 3 attempts. An attempt = one 60-second blitz round. You can buy unlimited entries.
3
Questions fire one after another for 60 seconds. Answer as many as possible. Some questions are double questions — read carefully, both parts must match your answer.
4
Questions are extremely hard — hashrate stats, power figures, rarity tiers, chain data, NFT IDs, and combined multi-fact queries.
5
Your highest score across all attempts in one entry is submitted to the leaderboard. The top 5 scores split the prize pool after a 5% platform fee.
6
Prize pool split: 🥇 40% · 🥈 25% · 🥉 15% · 4th 12% · 5th 8% of the post-fee pool.
7
Payments go to the admin treasury. Transaction hash is verified on-chain before your session starts — it cannot be faked or replayed.
🏆 LEADERBOARD & PROFILE
1
Connect your wallet and register a unique username to appear on the leaderboard. Usernames are permanent per address.
2
Your Profile page shows all your payment history, game sessions, and scores. Full audit trail.
3
The leaderboard shows the current prize pool (net of 5% fee) and how much each of the top 5 ranks will receive.
