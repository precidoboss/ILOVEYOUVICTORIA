What is ClipDAO?

ClipDAO is a decentralized bounty platform where streamers lock AVAX into smart contract escrow and clippers compete to make the best clips. No informal agreements. No waiting to get paid. Built specifically for Blaze and Arena streamers.

FAQ

Do I need crypto to use ClipDAO?
You need AVAX on Avalanche Fuji. New users get 1 free trial. To continue, mint an NFT for 0.1 AVAX (Early Access).
Which wallet?
Core Wallet (built for Avalanche) or MetaMask. ClipDAO auto-adds Fuji testnet.
Handle validation?
When you submit a clip, your registered X or YouTube handle should appear in the link. Set your handles in Profile settings.
Zero submissions bounty?
If zero submissions exist at deadline, you can cancel and your AVAX is fully refunded.
Where to post clips?
Post to X (Twitter) or YouTube, then submit that public link here. Clip must be from Blaze or Arena content.
